HAIR CARE

5 Simple Tips for Healthy Hair

Healthy hair begins with a consistent care routine. You do not need dozens of products to keep your hair looking beautiful. Small, regular habits can make a difference.

1. Keep Your Hair and Scalp Clean

Wash your hair regularly according to your hair type and lifestyle. Use a suitable shampoo to remove dirt, sweat and product buildup without irritating your scalp.

2. Moisturise Your Hair

Choose conditioners and moisturising products that work for your hair. Pay attention to how your hair responds and adjust your routine when necessary.

3. Handle Your Hair Gently

Avoid rough brushing and pulling. Detangle gently, starting near the ends and working upwards. Use a wide-tooth comb when appropriate.

4. Protect Your Hair at Night

A satin or silk bonnet or pillowcase can help reduce friction while sleeping. Keep longer hairstyles comfortably secured rather than pulling them tightly.

5. Avoid Excessive Heat

Frequent high heat can damage hair. When using heat-styling tools, use a suitable heat protectant and the lowest effective temperature.

Final Thoughts

Beautiful hair starts with patience, gentle handling and a routine that suits your needs. If you experience persistent scalp irritation or unusual hair loss, consider speaking with a qualified health professional.
