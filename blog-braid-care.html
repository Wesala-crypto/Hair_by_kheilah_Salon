How to Maintain Your Braids

Braids can be a convenient and beautiful hairstyle when installed and maintained properly. A gentle routine helps you care for your scalp and your natural hair while wearing the style.

1. Keep Your Scalp Clean

Clean your scalp gently and regularly. Follow the care instructions suited to your braid style, and make sure your hair and scalp dry properly after washing.

2. Moisturise When Needed

Use suitable lightweight products to help keep your natural hair moisturised. Avoid applying excessive amounts of heavy products that can accumulate around the scalp and braids.

3. Avoid Tight Braids

Braids should feel comfortable. Persistent pulling, pain or irritation can indicate excessive tension. Contact your stylist about loosening or removing an uncomfortable style.

4. Protect Your Braids While Sleeping

Consider using a satin or silk bonnet or pillowcase to reduce friction and help your braids stay neat for longer.

5. Be Gentle With Your Edges

Avoid repeatedly pulling, brushing or tightly styling the hair around your hairline. Gentle handling helps reduce unnecessary tension on fragile hairs.

6. Know When to Remove Them

Braids are not meant to stay in indefinitely. The suitable wear time depends on the style, your hair and its condition. Remove them if you notice persistent discomfort or damage, and detangle gently afterward.

Keep Your Hair Happy

Good braid care combines cleanliness, gentle handling and a comfortable installation. Ask your hairstylist for advice tailored to your hair and chosen style.
