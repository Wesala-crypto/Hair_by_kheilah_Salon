STYLE GUIDE

Choosing Your Next Hairstyle

Choosing a new hairstyle can be exciting. Whether you want something simple for everyday life or a special look for an event, the right style is one that makes you feel comfortable and confident.

1. Think About Your Lifestyle

Consider how much time you can spend styling and maintaining your hair. A low-maintenance style may suit a busy schedule, while other styles may need more frequent care.

2. Explore Different Styles

Braids, twists, cornrows, natural hairstyles and wigs all offer different possibilities. Collect inspiration photos and discuss the options with your hairstylist.

3. Consider Hair Care

Think about the maintenance your chosen style requires. Protective styles should still be comfortable and should not put excessive tension on your hair or scalp.

4. Plan for Your Occasion

Your everyday hairstyle may differ from a look for a wedding, celebration or formal event. Consider your outfit, activities and how long you need the style to last.

5. Communicate With Your Stylist

Bring reference photos and explain what you like about them. Ask about preparation, maintenance, appointment duration and the products you may need at home.

Your Style, Your Choice

Trends can provide inspiration, but you do not have to follow every trend. Choose a hairstyle that fits your preferences, comfort and routine.
